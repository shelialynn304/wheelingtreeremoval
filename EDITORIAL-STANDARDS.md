# Editorial Standards — Wheeling Tree Removal

## Purpose

Wheeling Tree Removal exists to help homeowners in Wheeling, WV and nearby Ohio Valley communities understand tree service needs and request quotes from local service providers.

Every page should help a real homeowner make a better decision before calling or submitting a quote request.

Do not publish generic local SEO filler.

## Core Content Rules

Each important page should include:

- A clear H1 that matches the page topic.
- A short intro that explains the homeowner problem.
- Local context when relevant.
- Practical information beyond obvious service claims.
- Clear call-to-action near the top and bottom.
- A working phone link.
- A working quote form or clear link to one.
- Honest explanation of what happens after a quote request.
- Safety warnings where relevant.
- No fake claims.

## Trust Rules

Do not claim:

- Licensed
- Insured
- Certified
- Arborist-reviewed
- Family-owned
- Locally owned
- Years of experience
- Number one
- Best
- Five-star rated
- Guaranteed lowest price

Unless that claim is verified and documented.

If contractor details are not finalized, use honest wording:

> Wheeling Tree Removal connects homeowners with local tree service providers. Quotes, scheduling, pricing, and work are handled by the service provider.

## People-First Content Rules

Content should answer real questions, such as:

- Is this tree dangerous?
- When is tree removal urgent?
- What affects the price?
- Can a tree near a house, fence, driveway, or power line be removed?
- Should the stump be removed too?
- What photos/details should the homeowner provide?
- What happens after submitting the form?
- What service areas are covered?

Avoid generic filler like:

> We provide professional tree removal in [CITY]. Call today for the best tree service.

That is not useful. It is SEO cardboard.

## Local Context

Use specific local context where natural:

- Wheeling hillsides
- Narrow driveways
- Older residential lots
- Storm damage
- Saturated soil
- Freeze-thaw damage
- Trees near homes, fences, roads, and power lines
- Ohio Valley service area

Do not awkwardly repeat city names.

## Safety Content

Never provide risky DIY removal instructions.

Include safety warnings for:

- Power lines
- Storm-damaged trees
- Hanging limbs
- Leaning trees
- Trees on homes, vehicles, roads, or driveways

Recommended wording:

> If a tree or limb is touching a power line, stay away and contact the utility company first.

## Quote Form Standards

Every main page should have either:

- A quote form with `id="quote"`, or
- A clear CTA linking to a quote form.

Every form should preserve:

```html
<form id="quote-form" action="https://formsubmit.co/realtorreadyhomeservices@gmail.com" method="POST">
```
