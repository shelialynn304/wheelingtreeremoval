# Editorial Standards — Wheeling Tree Removal

## Purpose

Wheeling Tree Removal exists to help homeowners in Wheeling, WV and nearby Ohio Valley communities understand tree service needs and request quotes from local service providers.

Every page should help a real homeowner make a better decision before calling or submitting a quote request.

Do not publish generic local SEO filler.

---

## Core Content Rules

Each important page should include:

- A clear H1 that matches the page topic.
- A short intro that explains the homeowner problem.
- Local context when relevant.
- Practical information beyond obvious service claims.
- Clear call-to-action near the top and bottom.
- A working phone link.
- A working quote form or clear link to one.
- Honest explanation of what happens after a quote request.
- Safety warnings where relevant.
- No fake claims.

---

## Trust Rules

Do not claim any of the following unless the claim is verified and documented:

- Licensed
- Insured
- Certified
- Arborist-reviewed
- Family-owned
- Locally owned
- Years of experience
- Number one
- Best
- Five-star rated
- Guaranteed lowest price
- Cheapest

If contractor details are not finalized, use honest wording:

"Wheeling Tree Removal connects homeowners with local tree service providers. Quotes, scheduling, pricing, and work are handled by the service provider."

Do not pretend the site is the actual contractor if it is functioning as a lead-generation or referral site.

---

## People-First Content Rules

Content should answer real homeowner questions, such as:

- Is this tree dangerous?
- When is tree removal urgent?
- What affects the price?
- Can a tree near a house, fence, driveway, road, or power line be removed?
- Should the stump be removed too?
- What photos or details should the homeowner provide?
- What happens after submitting the form?
- What service areas are covered?

Avoid generic filler like:

"We provide professional tree removal in [CITY]. Call today for the best tree service."

That is not useful. That is SEO cardboard.

---

## Local Context

Use specific local context where natural:

- Wheeling hillsides
- Narrow driveways
- Older residential lots
- Storm damage
- Saturated soil
- Freeze-thaw damage
- Trees near homes, fences, roads, and power lines
- Ohio Valley service area

Do not awkwardly repeat city names.

Good local content explains how local conditions affect the job, price, access, safety, or urgency.

---

## Safety Content

Never provide risky DIY tree removal instructions.

Include safety warnings for:

- Power lines
- Storm-damaged trees
- Hanging limbs
- Leaning trees
- Trees on homes, vehicles, roads, or driveways
- Cracked trunks
- Exposed roots
- Saturated soil around unstable trees

Recommended wording:

"If a tree or limb is touching a power line, stay away and contact the utility company first."

Do not encourage homeowners to cut, climb, pull, or move dangerous trees themselves.

---

## Quote Form Standards

Every main page should have either:

- A quote form with `id="quote-form"`, or
- A clear CTA linking to a quote form.

Every quote form should preserve this structure unless intentionally changed:

```html
<form id="quote-form" action="https://formsubmit.co/realtorreadyhomeservices@gmail.com" method="POST">
```

Do not break FormSubmit fields.

Recommended hidden fields:

```html
<input type="hidden" name="_subject" value="New Tree Service Lead">
<input type="hidden" name="_captcha" value="false">
<input type="hidden" name="_next" value="https://wheelingtreeremoval.com/thank-you.html">
<input type="hidden" name="_template" value="table">
<input type="hidden" name="_replyto" value="email">
```

Recommended visible fields:

- Name
- Phone
- Address or city
- Job type
- Description of the tree problem
- Urgency level, if relevant

---

## CTA Standards

Use clear calls-to-action:

- Call Now
- Get Free Quote
- Request a Free Estimate
- Estimate My Cost

Avoid hype:

- Best deal today
- Cheapest in town
- Guaranteed lowest price
- Number one tree experts
- Five-star tree pros
- Lowest price guaranteed

Every important page should make the next step obvious.

A visitor should never have to hunt for the phone number or quote form. Humans are lazy. Design accordingly.

---

## Location Page Standards

Each location page must have at least one unique paragraph specific to that community.

Do not copy the same city page and swap the city name.

Good location-page content should mention actual job conditions, such as:

- Tight lots
- Hillside yards
- Storm cleanup
- Mature trees
- Driveway access
- Nearby homes
- Nearby fences
- Nearby roads
- Nearby power lines

Example of weak location content:

"We provide tree removal in Bethlehem WV. Call today for professional tree service in Bethlehem WV."

Example of better location content:

"Tree removal in Bethlehem can involve sloped yards, older residential lots, and trees growing close to homes, fences, and driveways. Access, tree condition, storm damage, and nearby structures all affect how the job should be handled and priced."

## FAQ Standards

FAQs should answer real homeowner questions.

Good FAQ topics:

- What makes tree removal urgent?
- Can a leaning tree be saved?
- Is stump grinding required?
- Does tree removal cost more near a house?
- What information helps with a quote?
- What should I do if a tree hits a power line?
- How much does access affect price?
- Is a fallen tree always cheaper to remove?
- Should storm-damaged limbs be removed right away?

Avoid useless FAQs:

- Do you offer tree service?
- Are trees important?
- Should I call someone?
- Is tree removal a service?
- Do homeowners need yards?

If the answer is obvious to anyone with a pulse, it probably should not be an FAQ.

## SEO Standards

Each indexable page should have:

- One clear H1.
- Unique title tag.
- Unique meta description.
- Canonical URL using https://wheelingtreeremoval.com/.
- Internal links to related pages.
- Useful content that goes beyond generic service claims.
- No duplicate full-page content.
- No fake reviews.
- No fake schema.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No doorway-page sludge.

Use the apex domain:

https://wheelingtreeremoval.com/

Avoid using www in canonicals, sitemap URLs, internal links, or structured data unless the whole site is intentionally moved to www.

---

## Schema Standards

Only use schema that matches the real page and real business facts.

Acceptable schema types when accurate:

- LocalBusiness
- Service
- WebPage
- FAQPage
- BreadcrumbList

Do not add:

- Fake reviews
- Fake ratings
- Fake aggregateRating
- Fake founder information
- Fake awards
- Fake certifications
- Fake business addresses
- Fake opening hours

If contractor details are not finalized, keep schema conservative.

---

## Tracking Standards

Do not add duplicate inline tracking scripts.

Central tracking belongs in:

tracking.js

Phone clicks, quote clicks, and form submit attempts should be tracked once.

Recommended events:

- phone_click
- quote_button_click
- quote_form_submit_attempt
- generate_lead

The real form conversion should be tracked on:

thank-you.html

Do not count every quote button click as a final lead. A click is not a lead. A click is a person poking the glass.

---

## Form Conversion Standards

A real quote form lead should be counted when the user reaches:

thank-you.html

Form submit tracking can be used as a submit attempt, but the thank-you page should be treated as the stronger conversion signal.

All quote forms should redirect to:

https://wheelingtreeremoval.com/thank-you.html

---

## Content Quality Standards

Before publishing, ask:

- Does this page help a real homeowner?
- Does it answer questions people actually have?
- Does it include local context?
- Does it avoid fake claims?
- Does it explain what affects price or urgency?
- Does it explain what happens next?
- Is the page specific enough to deserve existing?
- Would a homeowner trust this page enough to call?

If the page only exists to rank for a city keyword, rewrite it.

---

## Tone Standards

Use a tone that is:

- Clear
- Practical
- Local
- Trustworthy
- Direct
- Easy to understand

Avoid:

- Corporate sludge
- Over-hype
- Fake urgency
- Excessive jokes
- Keyword stuffing
- Vague promises
- Robotic AI phrasing

A little personality is fine. Turning every paragraph into a stand-up routine is not.

---

## Claims That Need Proof

These claims require proof before publishing:

- Licensed
- Insured
- Bonded
- Certified arborist
- ISA certified
- Locally owned
- Family owned
- Veteran owned
- In business since [year]
- Serving the area for [number] years
- Five-star rated
- Best rated
- Number one
- Lowest price
- Guaranteed cheapest
- Emergency service available 24/7

If proof is not available, use safer wording.

Safer wording examples:

- Local tree service help
- Serving Wheeling and nearby Ohio Valley communities
- Request a free quote
- Fast response options may be available
- Final pricing depends on the provider and job details

---

## Internal Linking Standards

Pages should link naturally to related pages.

Examples:

- Tree removal page links to stump grinding.
- Emergency tree removal page links to tree removal and quote form.
- Cost calculator page links to tree removal, stump grinding, and quote form.
- Homepage links to major services and location pages.
- Location pages link back to core service pages.

Avoid excessive exact-match anchor text.

Good anchors:

- tree removal services
- stump grinding
- emergency tree cleanup
- estimate your tree service cost
- request a free quote

Bad anchors:

- best tree removal Wheeling WV cheap tree removal Wheeling WV

That is not an anchor. That is a cry for help.

---

## Image Standards

Images should have descriptive alt text.

Good alt text:

Storm damaged tree near a house in Wheeling WV

Bad alt text:

tree service best cheap professional Wheeling WV tree removal

Use real job photos when available.

Do not label AI-generated or stock images as real completed jobs.

Use compressed images where possible.

---

## Page Experience Standards

Pages should be easy to use on mobile.

Check:

- Phone button is visible.
- Quote CTA is easy to find.
- Form fields are readable.
- Buttons are large enough to tap.
- Popups do not block the main CTA.
- Sticky call button does not cover form submit buttons.
- Content is scannable with headings, lists, and short paragraphs.

Mobile users are usually impatient, distracted, and possibly standing in a yard staring at a tree that just tried to murder a garage. Make the page simple.

---

## Pre-Publish Checklist

Before publishing a page, verify:

- The page helps a real homeowner.
- The main CTA works.
- The quote form works.
- The phone link works.
- The page has no fake claims.
- The content is not copied across location pages.
- The title and H1 are descriptive.
- The page includes practical pricing, safety, or process information where relevant.
- The page has a canonical URL.
- The page does not sound like mass-produced SEO filler.
- The page includes honest service-provider language if contractor details are not finalized.
- The page has useful internal links.
- The page works on mobile.

---

## Final Rule

Do not publish content just because it contains keywords.

Publish content because it helps a homeowner decide what to do next.

If the page does not help, it is not content. It is clutter with metadata.
